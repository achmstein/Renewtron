Renew Your Business Name

Keep your business name active by renewing your registration with ASIC. Quick and easy online renewal process.

Key Points
  • You must renew your business name in order to continue carrying on a business using that name.
  • Registration can be renewed for 1 or 3 year periods.
  • If you no longer need your business name, it should be cancelled.
